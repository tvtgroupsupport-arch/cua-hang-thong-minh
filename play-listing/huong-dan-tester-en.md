# Tester instructions (English) — for App Tester Community posts

**BEPO - Quản lý cửa hàng** — a point-of-sale & inventory app for retail shops (Vietnamese UI, works offline).

Thank you for testing! Please join the test with the opt-in link (`<OPT-IN LINK>`) and install the app from Google Play. On first launch, tick **"Nạp dữ liệu mẫu" (load sample data)** and tap **"Bắt đầu sử dụng"** — the app fills itself with 30 days of demo products and sales, so you can try everything without typing in data. Over the next few days, please try: **selling** (add items to the cart, cash / QR / on-account payment), **barcode scanning** (camera button), **products & stock** (add an item, receive stock), **customers & debts**, **reports** (tap the bar chart, export to Excel), and **More → Gói cước & tài khoản** (subscription screen; purchases made with a Google test account are free). Use the app on different days so it counts as continuous testing. If you find a bug, a crash or anything confusing, please email **tvtgroup.support@gmail.com** (or reply in the community thread) with your phone model, Android version, what you did and a screenshot. Finally, if you enjoyed the app, please leave an honest rating and a short review on its Google Play page — it really helps a small developer. Thank you!
